Privacy Policy

Protecting your personal data matters to us. We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP) and, where applicable, the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

Amanthos Hotel AG — Hotel Chalet Swiss
Seestrasse 22, 3800 Interlaken, Switzerland
Email: chaletswiss@amanthos.com · Phone: +41 41 562 76 60

2. What data we process

Bookings: When you book a room through our website, we process the data you provide (first name, surname, email address, phone number, travel dates, selected room category and extras). This data is required to conclude and perform the accommodation contract.

Payments: Payments are handled through a secure payment link provided by our payment service provider Adyen. Your card details are processed exclusively by Adyen and never reach our systems.

Website use: When you visit this website, our hosting provider processes technical access data (such as IP address, date and time, browser type) in server logs, to the extent required for operation and security.

Local storage and cookies: We always use technically necessary entries in your browser's local storage (language preference, cookie notice). In addition, the analytics and marketing services listed under section 4 set cookies, subject to your consent.

3. Error and performance monitoring

This website uses Sentry, a service provided by Functional Software, Inc., to detect technical errors and measure loading performance. Processing takes place in the provider's EU region. We collect error messages with technical details about browser and device, as well as loading speed metrics. IP addresses, cookies and form contents are not transmitted.

If an error occurs, the course of the affected session is additionally recorded so that the cause can be traced. Text, input and image content are fully masked in the process. No recording takes place without an error.

4. Audience measurement and marketing

Plausible: We use Plausible Analytics, provided by Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia), to measure how the site is used. Plausible works without cookies and without cross-device recognition. No personal data and no complete IP addresses are stored; evaluation is carried out exclusively in aggregated form.

Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads: Google Analytics and Google Ads features provided by Google Ireland Limited are embedded via Google Tag Manager. These services set cookies and collect information about your visit in order to evaluate use of the website and measure the effectiveness of advertising.

5. Recipients and processors

We use the following service providers, which process personal data on our behalf, to operate the website and handle bookings:

  • Apaleo GmbH, Munich (Germany) — property management system in which your booking is held
  • Adyen N.V. (Netherlands) — payment processing
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) — website hosting (GitHub Pages)
  • Render Services, Inc. (USA) — hosting of the booking interface
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) — error and performance monitoring, processed in the EU region
  • Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia) — cookie-free audience measurement
  • Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC (USA) — Tag Manager, Analytics, Ads as well as the Google Maps mapping service and Google Fonts, which are loaded from Google servers when the website is opened

Where service providers are based outside Switzerland or the EEA, transfers take place on the basis of appropriate safeguards (in particular standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions).

6. Retention

We retain personal data for as long as it is required to handle the booking and to meet statutory obligations (in particular commercial and tax retention obligations, generally 10 years).

7. Your rights

You have the right to obtain information about the personal data processed about you, as well as the right to rectification, erasure and data portability, unless statutory obligations preclude this. Please contact chaletswiss@amanthos.com.

8. Changes

We may amend this privacy policy at any time. The version published on this website at the relevant time applies.
